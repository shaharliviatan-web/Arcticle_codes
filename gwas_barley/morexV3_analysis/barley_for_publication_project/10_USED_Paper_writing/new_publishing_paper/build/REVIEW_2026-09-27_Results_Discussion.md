## 3. Organisation of the Discussion (redrafted; the "limitations before β-glucan" rule is dropped)

**3.1 The problems now**
- **§3 mixes two topics.** ¶1–¶2 are genetic architecture (heritability, rare alleles); ¶3–¶4 are about
  choosing genes. It also ends on Sariel's closing line ("These four genes therefore identify distinct
  biological pathways…") in the middle of the Discussion.
- **§3 ¶4 doesn't hold together.** It opens "Two of the candidate genes connect to further evidence",
  gives a sentence each to PHT4;3 and the GDSL gene, then concludes "These **four** genes therefore…".
  GPAT6 and GH17 have no sentence, so "therefore" doesn't follow. Your rule from 1.11 applies here: all
