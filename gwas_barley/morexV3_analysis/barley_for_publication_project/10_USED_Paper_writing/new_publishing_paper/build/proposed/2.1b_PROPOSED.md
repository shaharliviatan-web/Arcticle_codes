**Reads after approval:**

> Candidate genes were further evaluated by haplotype analysis to identify phenotypic divergence between accessions carrying contrasting alleles across haplotype groups. Gene-level haplotype analysis tested whether variation across each gene was associated with the corresponding trait (Kruskal–Wallis, Benjamini–Hochberg correction within each trait, *q* ≤ 0.05) and quantified the direction and magnitude of differences among haplotype groups, information not provided by the single-SNP tests. Of the 55 candidate genes, 30 could be tested and 23 were significant, distributed over 12 of the 36 loci. The 25 genes that could not be tested lacked the variation needed to define haplotypes: 21 of them carried five or fewer SNPs in their window and seven carried none at all. Within traits, all six testable β-glucan genes and all five testable starch genes were significant, against 12 of 19 for fiber, whereas none of the six protein genes could be tested.
