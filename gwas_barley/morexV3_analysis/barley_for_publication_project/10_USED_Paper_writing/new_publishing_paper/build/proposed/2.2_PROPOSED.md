**With the changes marked:**

To ask which of these haplotypes are present in cultivated barley, five European spring malting cultivars released between 2012 and 2018 were genotyped across the same three windows from an independent MorexV3 call set and drawn beneath the wild haplotype groups (Fig. 4d–f; Online Resource 3 <sup>(TODO 306)</sup>). At the acyltransferase the five cultivars matched the two low-fiber haplotype groups closely, at 95–98% and 88–91% of called sites, and the high-fiber group<del style="color:#c0392b;background:#fdecea">, which carries the entire signal at this gene,</del> at only 39–45%, so the high-fiber wild haplotype is essentially absent from these lines.
