\vspace{1mm}{\small \textbf{Fig. S1} Decay of linkage disequilibrium (LD) with physical distance in the 290 wild barley accessions: \textbf{a} across the genome; \textbf{b} per chromosome. The r² of every pair of the 7,110,996 SNPs within 2.5 Mb (pairs with r² ≥ 0.001) was computed in PLINK v1.90, averaged in 1-kb distance bins (gray points) and smoothed with a LOESS curve (span 0.10) weighted by the number of pairs per bin (red line). Dashed line, r² = 0.2; dotted line in \textbf{a}, the distance at which the smoothed genome-wide r² falls to 0.2 (about 188 kb)\par}
