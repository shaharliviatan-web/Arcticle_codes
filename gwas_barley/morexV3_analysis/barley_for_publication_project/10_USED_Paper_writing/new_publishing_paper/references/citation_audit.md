**Tiers**

- **A**: a specific fact, number, gene function or mechanism. Checked against the full text, with the exact passage and
  its page (or section, for XML full texts).
- **B**: background. Checked against the abstract, or the full text when free.
- **C**: tool, database or statistical method. Only checked as the right citation for the tool and version
  (bibliographic check, `bibliography_check.md`). The 2 tier-C works with a specific claim (Jayakodi 2024, König 2023)
  were downloaded too.

**Verdicts** (step 5, filled in later): ✅ supported · ⚠️ partly supported or worded more strongly than the source ·
❌ not supported. A source from another species, cited as such, is fine; a mismatch is flagged only when it makes the
sentence wrong.
