[G] HOW THE FIGURES ARE BUILT
  Group rows are a PER-SNP MAJORITY CONSENSUS over the group's members,
  ignoring missing calls; an exact 50/50 tie resolves to the REFERENCE allele
  when REF is one of the tied states (otherwise missing). A consensus is
  used rather than one representative accession because wild missingness is
  high, so a single plant's row would show grey tiles that are artefacts of its
  sequencing rather than features of the haplotype.
  Elite lines are NOT assigned to a haplotype group anywhere: crosshap never
  saw them. The barcodes are aligned so the comparison is left to the reader.
  Three SNP-matching versions are produced (shared_sites, filled_marked, filled_silent).
  Barcode rows are separated by white gaps; each legend lists only the states drawn.

[H] CAVEAT FOR THE MANUSCRIPT
  The figure shows which haplotypes elite lines carry; it does not by itself
  show that breeding selected them.
